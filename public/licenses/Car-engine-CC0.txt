http://www.pdsounds.org/sounds/start_up_car_with_seat_belt_warning_beep

public-domain source. The current download is offered under CC0.

Stored unchanged as public/audio/car-engine.wav. At playback, Slipstream
crossfades the loop boundary, normalizes it, filters harsh frequencies, and
varies playback speed and gain with driving input. No synthesized engine layer.
