The font licenses were checked in the installed packages. OFL permits embedding and commercial use with the supplied notices. Complete copies are retained in the repository and deployed with the static build. Only Latin font subsets and used weights are bundled. All team names, signage, and circuit names here are fictional.

The engine WAV is bundled locally (76,332 bytes); no third-party audio service is needed during play. The source page was checked on 2026-10-01 and identifies CC0 and the public-domain recording provenance. The original WAV is unmodified on disk; playback applies a 40 ms loop crossfade, normalization, low-pass filtering, and speed/gain modulation.

Pit garages, painted stopping boxes, pit crew, tyre props and service animations are original procedural work in `src/track/PitVisual.ts`. The expanded circuit and pit-lane geometry are generated locally; no downloaded racing-team assets or official Formula 1 branding are used.
